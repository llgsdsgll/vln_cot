Spot in simulator is an agile, quadrupedal robot. 
Action: Spot support three kinds of action: move_forward, turn_left and turn_right.
Sensors: Spot is equipped with three RGB cameras at a height of 0.5 meters in front, left and right to obtain embodied images in these three directions.
Mobility: Spot’s four-legged design allows it to navigate challenging terrains, including stairs, rocky surfaces, and cluttered environments.
Use: As spot is shaped like a dog, it can do practical navigation work for human in domestic scenes and approach objects from a relatively low viewpoint.
